# CaseCompass Features

1. **Automated Document Parsing**
   - Ingests raw text, court filings, depositions, and notices.
   - Cleans boilerplates, citations, and court headers.

2. **Temporal & Entity Extraction**
   - Extracts structured dates (ISO format) from legal boilerplate expressions.
   - Matches procedural actors, presiding judges, and filing parties.

3. **Intelligent Timeline Assembly**
   - Chronological visualization of all case milestones.
   - Filtering by date range, document source, and milestone urgency.

4. **Deadline Radar**
   - Flags court-imposed deadlines (e.g., "within 14 days of this order").
   - Categorizes by overdue, urgent (< 48 hrs), and upcoming.

5. **Contradiction & Conflict Engine**
   - Identifies conflicting statements or dates across multiple legal exhibits.

6. **Missing Evidence & Gap Detection**
   - Flags mentions of exhibits, past orders, or appendices not present in the record.

7. **Audit-Ready Source Viewer**
   - One-click tracing of any timeline event directly back to source lines.
